They ruled Egypt. Then their bodies disappeared from the tombs built to protect them.

Hidden in the cliffs above the Nile, the Royal Cache at Deir el-Bahari (TT320 / DB320) preserved some of Ancient Egypt's most famous rulers — including Ramesses II and Seti I. Why were they moved? Who protected them? And what happened when the burial was revealed in 1881?

Travel from the Valley of the Kings to the secret mountain shaft, following the priests of Amun, the Abd el-Rassul family, the archaeological discoveries of 1881, and the remarkable afterlife of the royal mummies. The story also reaches Cairo's 2021 Pharaohs' Golden Parade — which included mummies associated with more than one ancient cache.

This original illustrated documentary explores preservation, tomb robbery, reburial, and the difficult questions historians still debate. Narration and artwork bring the story to life; scenes are artistic reconstructions rather than archaeological footage.

HISTORICAL NOTES
• The famous goat-discovery account is a traditional version of events, not a conclusively verified fact.
• The extent and motives of ancient removal of precious coffin materials remain subjects of scholarly debate.
• The 2021 parade included royal mummies from multiple caches, not TT320 alone.
