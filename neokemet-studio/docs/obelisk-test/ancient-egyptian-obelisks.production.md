# ANCIENT EGYPTIAN OBELISKS
**Target:** 9:16 and 16:9

## Production scenes

**Scene 01 — Born in Granite**

**Voiceover**
In Aswan, quarry workers outlined an obelisk in the granite bedrock.

**Visual:** Wide Aswan granite quarry with one unfinished obelisk outlined in bedrock. Heading "BORN IN GRANITE".
**Sound / transition:** Hard cut.

**Scene 02 — Cut from the Rock**

**Voiceover**
Stone pounders chipped a deep trench around the unfinished obelisk.

**Visual:** Close workers carving a trench around the obelisk with dolerite pounding stones. Label "CUT FROM THE ROCK".
**Sound / transition:** Hard cut.

**Scene 03 — Shaped and Smoothed**

**Voiceover**
Craftsmen carefully smoothed the long faces of the granite shaft.

**Visual:** Horizontal obelisk resting on supports while craftsmen smooth its surfaces. Label "SHAPED AND SMOOTHED".
**Sound / transition:** Hard cut.

**Scene 04 — Dragged on a Sledge**

**Voiceover**
Teams hauled the massive shaft overland on a wooden sledge.

**Visual:** Workers pulling the horizontal obelisk on a wooden sledge with taut ropes. Label "DRAGGED ON A SLEDGE".
**Sound / transition:** Hard cut.

**Scene 05 — Down to the Nile**

**Voiceover**
The stone was brought to the riverbank for loading onto a barge.

**Visual:** At the riverbank an obelisk is maneuvered up a loading ramp onto a barge. Label "DOWN TO THE NILE".
**Sound / transition:** Hard cut.

**Scene 06 — North by River**

**Voiceover**
The Nile provided a route for carrying the monument north.

**Visual:** River barge sailing with a long granite obelisk along the Nile. Label "NORTH BY RIVER".
**Sound / transition:** Hard cut.

**Scene 07 — Raised to the Sky**

**Voiceover**
Teams raised the obelisk upright in a difficult feat of engineering.

**Visual:** Illustrative reconstruction of an obelisk tilted upright with ropes and earthen support. Label "RAISED TO THE SKY".
**Sound / transition:** Hard cut.

**Scene 08 — A Ray in Stone**

**Voiceover**
At the temple, the completed obelisk stood as a monument of power and light.

**Visual:** Completed upright obelisk beside a temple pylon with people for scale. Heading "A RAY IN STONE".
**Sound / transition:** Hard cut.
