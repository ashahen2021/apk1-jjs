# THE MAKING OF AN OBELISK
**Target:** 9:16 and 16:9

Test film for the Obelisk template: eight stages of the work, from the granite
of Aswan to the temple court. Imported with the ordinary production-script
importer; nothing below is hand-edited after import.

## Production scenes

**Scene 01 — The Granite of Aswan**

**Voiceover**
Every obelisk began as part of a granite hill at Aswan.

**Visual:** The granite quarry at Aswan: rounded outcrops above the river, an obelisk marked out in the rock and half cut free, quarrymen at work along it. Heading "CUT FROM ONE STONE".
**Sound / transition:** Wind over stone.

**Scene 02 — Pounding the Trench**

**Voiceover**
Copper could not cut granite, so the men pounded it away with balls of dolerite.

**Visual:** Close in the trench beside the obelisk: workers stand in the cut and pound the bedrock with dolerite balls, dust rising at every blow. Label: "POUNDED OUT OF THE ROCK".
**Sound / transition:** Stone on stone.

**Scene 03 — Smoothing the Faces**

**Voiceover**
Freed from the rock, its four faces were rubbed smooth and cut with the king's names.

**Visual:** The obelisk lies on timber baulks under a linen awning while men polish its faces smooth with rubbing stones and sand, and a sculptor carves the hieroglyphs. Label: "SMOOTHED AND INSCRIBED".
**Sound / transition:** Stone rubbing on stone.

**Scene 04 — Hauled to the River**

**Voiceover**
Then hundreds of men dragged it to the river.

**Visual:** The obelisk lashed to a great wooden sledge on rollers, hauled along a track by gangs of men on long ropes while water is poured under the runners. Label: "HAULED BY HAND".
**Sound / transition:** Men heaving in time.

**Scene 05 — Onto the Barge**

**Voiceover**
At the riverbank it was drawn aboard a barge built for it alone.

**Visual:** At the embankment the obelisk is loaded onto a great barge moored at the riverbank: a gang on deck draws it aboard while men with levers ease it off the quay. Label: "ONTO THE BARGE".
**Sound / transition:** Timber creaking.

**Scene 06 — Down the Nile**

**Voiceover**
Towed by lines of rowers, the barge carried it down the Nile.

**Visual:** The laden barge carries the obelisk down the Nile, towed by boats with oars; the temple it is bound for stands on the far bank. Label: "DOWN THE NILE".
**Sound / transition:** Oars in water.

**Scene 07 — Raised Upright**

**Voiceover**
At the temple it was let down through sand onto its base, and hauled upright.

**Visual:** The obelisk is erected: tilted off the end of a brick ramp, its foot lowered through a sand-filled pit onto the pedestal while a gang on ropes pulls it upright. Label: "RAISED UPRIGHT".
**Sound / transition:** Ropes straining.

**Scene 08 — Before the Temple**

**Voiceover**
And there it stood, its golden point catching the first light of the sun.

**Visual:** The completed obelisk stands with its pair before the temple pylon, gilded pyramidion gleaming, priests small at its foot. Heading "FOR THE SUN". No unrelated end card. Rise.
**Sound / transition:** A held chord.
