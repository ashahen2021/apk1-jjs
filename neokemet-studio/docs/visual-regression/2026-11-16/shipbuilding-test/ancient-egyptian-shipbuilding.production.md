## Production scenes

**Scene 01 — The Shipyard**

**Voiceover**
Along the Nile, Egypt built the boats that carried a kingdom.

**Visual:** An Egyptian shipyard beside the Nile: a hull on the stocks, stacked timber, a reed shelter, the river behind. Heading "BUILT ON THE NILE".
**Sound / transition:** River ambience.

**Scene 02 — Timber Arrives**

**Voiceover**
Good timber was scarce, so cedar logs came in by water.

**Visual:** A boatyard bank where cedar logs are unloaded from a river boat and carried ashore to the timber pile. Label: "CEDAR FROM AFAR".
**Sound / transition:** Water against a hull.

**Scene 03 — Shaping the Timber**

**Voiceover**
Carpenters shaped every plank by hand.

**Visual:** Craftsmen shaping wooden hull components: one works a plank with an adze, another is sawing; finished curved pieces wait beside them. Label: "SHAPED BY HAND".
**Sound / transition:** Adze on wood.

**Scene 04 — Keel and Ribs**

**Voiceover**
First came the keel, then the ribs that gave the hull its form.

**Visual:** The keel and ribs of a hull standing on the stocks like a skeleton; a rib is offered up and driven home. Label: "KEEL AND RIBS".
**Sound / transition:** Mallet blows.

**Scene 05 — Bound With Rope**

**Voiceover**
Planks were lashed together with rope, not nailed.

**Visual:** Workers assembling a vessel using ropes and wooden tools: lashings pulled tight over the planked hull, mallets driving the joints home. Label: "BOUND WITH ROPE".
**Sound / transition:** Rope creaking.

**Scene 06 — Raising the Mast**

**Voiceover**
Then the mast was hauled upright.

**Visual:** A gang hoists the mast of the finished hull upright with a long rope; the furled sail waits on the ground. Label: "RAISING THE MAST".
**Sound / transition:** Men heaving in time.

**Scene 07 — The Launch**

**Voiceover**
At last the hull slid down into the river.

**Visual:** Launch day: the finished hull slides down the slipway into the water while the yard pushes from behind. Label: "INTO THE RIVER".
**Sound / transition:** A great splash.

**Scene 08 — Under Sail**

**Voiceover**
And a new boat took the wind on the Nile.

**Visual:** A completed cargo boat sailing on the Nile, sail set, cargo on deck, helmsman at the steering oar. Heading "UNDER SAIL". No unrelated end card.
**Sound / transition:** Wind in the sail.
