In April 1939 the BBC broadcast Tutankhamun's trumpets to millions of listeners, and months later the world was at war: the legend of the cursed trumpet was born. This documentary follows the sound itself: what the two trumpets from KV62 can physically play, what happened on the night of the broadcast, why the curse falls apart under scrutiny, and the ancient source, Plutarch, that records Egyptians refusing the trumpet because it sounded like the animal of Seth. It ends inside an Egyptian temple, with the chantresses, harps and sistra that took its place.
