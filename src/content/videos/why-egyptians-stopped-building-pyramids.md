Not one royal pyramid has ever been found with its king still inside. This documentary follows the programme from Imhotep's Step Pyramid, through the collapse at Meidum and the correction on the Bent Pyramid, to the peak at Giza — then the booby-trapped mudbrick pyramids of the Middle Kingdom, the hidden cliff tombs of the Valley of the Kings, and the pyramids that carried on in Nubia long after Egypt gave up.
