Before air conditioning, the people of ancient Egypt still had to get through the hottest nights of the year. This calm sleep documentary looks at how they did it: flat roofs and reed mats, thick mud-brick walls, openings facing the north breeze, porous water jars, headrests and fine linen, fans and oil lamps, and the tomb builders of Deir el-Medina in their huts above the Valley of the Kings.
