Instead of arguing over theories, this documentary follows a single block of white casing limestone from the quarries of Tura to the face of the Great Pyramid, and looks at the evidence each stage left behind: copper tools, the wet-sand sledge of Djehutihotep, the ramp at Hatnub, the logbook of Merer, the Nile branch that reached Giza, the workers' town, the precision of the base and what happened to the casing.
