For five hundred years the kings of the New Kingdom were buried in one dry valley on the west bank of the Nile, in corridors cut by hand deep into the limestone. This documentary follows the men who cut them, the crew of Deir el-Medina: why the pyramids were given up, why the natural pyramid of el-Qurn watched over the valley, how a tomb was planned on papyrus, cut by lamplight with copper and bronze chisels and painted with the books of the underworld, and why almost every tomb was robbed except one.
