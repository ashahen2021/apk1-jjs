Every night, the ancient Egyptians believed, the sun sailed through the Duat, the hidden world beneath the earth, and rose again at dawn. This sleep documentary walks through those twelve hours and through the spells ordinary Egyptians carried into the same darkness: the Pyramid Texts of Unas, the Coffin Texts, the oval burial chamber of Thutmose III with the whole night painted on its walls, the weighing of the heart, the shabtis and the Field of Reeds.
