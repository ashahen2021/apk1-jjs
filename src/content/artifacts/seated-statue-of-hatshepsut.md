---
name: Seated Statue of Hatshepsut
material: Indurated limestone with traces of paint
dimensions: 195 cm high
dynasty: "18"
pharaoh: hatshepsut
foundAt: Deir el-Bahari, western Thebes
museum: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York
description: This life-size statue shows Hatshepsut as pharaoh, blending a king's regalia with feminine features. It is now in the Metropolitan Museum.
---

Hatshepsut wears the nemes headcloth and royal kilt of a king, but the delicate face and slender body keep a feminine character, reflecting an early stage of her reign.

The statue was smashed and thrown into a quarry after her death. The Metropolitan Museum's excavations in the 1920s recovered the fragments, which were then reassembled.
