---
name: Bust of Nefertiti
material: Painted limestone and gypsum, with a quartz inlay eye
dimensions: 48 cm high
dynasty: "18"
pharaoh: akhenaten
foundAt: Workshop of the sculptor Thutmose, Amarna, by Ludwig Borchardt's team (1912)
museum: Neues Museum, Berlin
description: The painted bust of Nefertiti, found at Amarna in 1912, is an icon of ancient beauty and of the Amarna art revolution.
---

Found in the workshop of the royal sculptor Thutmose, the bust was probably a studio model for other portraits of Akhenaten's queen. Only one eye was ever inlaid.

It has been in Berlin since 1913, and Egypt has repeatedly asked for its return.
