name: Narmer Palette
material: Siltstone
dimensions: 64 cm high
dynasty: "1"
foundAt: Hierakonpolis (Nekhen), by James Quibell and Frederick Green (1897–98)
museum: Egyptian Museum, Cairo
description: The Narmer Palette shows King Narmer wearing the crowns of Upper and Lower Egypt and is one of the earliest historical records of Egypt.
---

This ceremonial palette, a large version of those used to grind cosmetics, shows King Narmer wearing the white crown on one side and the red crown on the other.

It was long read as a record of the unification of Egypt. Many Egyptologists now see it as a symbolic statement of royal power, but it remains one of the earliest monuments to name a king.
