For nearly 3,800 years the Great Pyramid was the tallest human-made structure on Earth. Its sides are aligned to the cardinal directions with an error of only a fraction of a degree.

Inside, the Grand Gallery climbs to the King's Chamber, built of red Aswan granite and roofed with granite beams weighing tens of tons. Workers' settlements excavated south of the plateau show a well-fed, organised workforce rather than slaves.
