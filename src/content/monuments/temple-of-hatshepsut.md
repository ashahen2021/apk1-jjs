Designed, according to tradition, by the official **Senenmut**, the temple rises in three colonnaded terraces set against a sheer limestone cliff, next to the older temple of Mentuhotep II.

Its reliefs record the divine birth of Hatshepsut and the celebrated expedition to the land of **Punt**, showing ships loaded with incense trees, ebony and exotic animals.
