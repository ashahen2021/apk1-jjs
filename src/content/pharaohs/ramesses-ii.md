The third king of the 19th Dynasty, Ramesses II outlived many of his children and became, in later memory, the model of what a pharaoh should be. Nine later kings took his name.

Kadesh ended in a stalemate that his temples present as a triumph, but the conflict led to a lasting peace treaty with the Hittites, copies of which survive in both Egyptian and Hittite. His building programme — from **Abu Simbel** in Nubia to the Ramesseum at Thebes — is unmatched.
