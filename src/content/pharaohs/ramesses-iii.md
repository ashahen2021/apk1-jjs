Ramesses III fought off the migrating **Sea Peoples** in the eighth year of his reign, and his mortuary temple at Medinet Habu preserves the only large-scale depiction of a naval battle from the ancient Near East.

Economic strain showed later: in his 29th year the workmen of Deir el-Medina laid down their tools over late rations. A CT scan of his mummy in 2012 revealed a deep cut to the throat, confirming that the harem conspiracy against him succeeded.
