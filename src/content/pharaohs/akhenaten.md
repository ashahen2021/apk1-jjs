Born Amenhotep IV, he changed his name to **Akhenaten** — "effective for the Aten" — and made the sun disc the centre of state religion. The temples of Amun were closed and the god's name was hacked from monuments.

At **Amarna** he built an entirely new city in a few years. After his death the experiment was abandoned, his city emptied and later kings omitted him from their lists of rulers. The letters of the Amarna archive, however, preserve a unique record of diplomacy with Babylon, Mitanni and the Hittites.
