Menkaure, a son of **[Khafre](/pharaohs/khafre/)**, built the third pyramid on the [Giza Plateau](/monuments/giza-plateau/). It is far smaller than the Great Pyramid, but its lowest sixteen courses were cased in granite, part of which was left with a rough, undressed surface: the casing was never finished.

His temples were completed in mud brick after his death. Excavating the valley temple in 1908–10, George Reisner's expedition found celebrated sculpture, including the pair statue of the king with a queen and a series of triads showing Menkaure with the goddess Hathor and provincial deities. The workers' town excavated by AERA south-east of the Sphinx was in use during the 4th Dynasty building programme.
