A Theban ruler of the 11th Dynasty, Mentuhotep II completed the long war against the kings of Herakleopolis and reunited Egypt around 2055 BCE. Changes to his titles over his reign trace the progress of that reunification.

His terraced mortuary temple at **Deir el-Bahari** was unlike anything built before, and five centuries later it inspired the architects of Hatshepsut's temple next door.
