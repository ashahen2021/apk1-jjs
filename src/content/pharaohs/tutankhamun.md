Born Tutankhaten, probably a son of Akhenaten, the young king changed his name to **Tutankhamun** as the court returned to Thebes and Memphis and the old gods were restored.

His short reign would be a footnote if not for **KV62**. Discovered in November 1922, it was the only royal tomb in the Valley of the Kings found largely intact, and its treasures — the gold mask above all — shaped the modern image of ancient Egypt.
