Pepi II became king as a young boy and ruled for more than six decades — Manetho credits him with 94 years. His letter to the explorer **Harkhuf**, preserved on the walls of Harkhuf's tomb at Aswan, is one of the most human documents of the Old Kingdom.

Over his long reign, provincial governors grew wealthy and independent. When he died, the centralised pyramid state he had inherited did not long survive him.
