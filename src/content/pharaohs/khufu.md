Son of Sneferu, the most prolific pyramid builder in Egyptian history, Khufu inherited both the know-how and the organisation to attempt something without precedent. The result was the **Great Pyramid**, originally about 146 metres tall and built from roughly 2.3 million blocks.

Papyri found at Wadi el-Jarf on the Red Sea coast — the oldest inscribed papyri known — record an official named Merer shipping limestone from Tura to Giza for Khufu's project, giving a rare first-hand glimpse of the workforce behind the pyramid.
