Known in his own time by his Horus name **Netjerikhet**, Djoser ruled from Memphis at the dawn of the Old Kingdom. His reign is remembered for one revolutionary idea: building for eternity in stone rather than mud brick.

His vizier and architect **Imhotep** stacked six mastaba-like platforms on top of one another at Saqqara, creating the Step Pyramid and a vast walled funerary complex around it. Later generations remembered Imhotep as a sage, and by the Late Period he was worshipped as a god of healing.
