Cut high in a cleft of the cliffs and reached by a steep stair, KV34 was designed to be hidden. Its oval burial chamber is painted with the complete **Amduat**, the guide to the sun god's night journey, drawn in stick-like figures as if on papyrus.

The king's quartzite sarcophagus remains in the chamber; his mummy had been moved in antiquity to the royal cache at Deir el-Bahari.
