Built for the favourite wife of Ramesses II, QV66 is often called the Sistine Chapel of ancient Egypt. Its walls show Nefertari guided by gods through the afterlife on a background of brilliant white plaster and a ceiling of golden stars.

A conservation project by the Getty Conservation Institute and the Egyptian Antiquities Organization in 1986–1992 stabilised the fragile paintings.
