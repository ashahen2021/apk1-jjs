Small for a royal tomb, KV62 may originally have been prepared for someone else and adapted after the king's early death. Only the burial chamber is decorated, with scenes including the Opening of the Mouth ceremony.

Hidden beneath debris from the later tomb of Ramesses VI, it escaped the systematic robberies of antiquity. Clearing its four rooms took Howard Carter's team about ten years.
