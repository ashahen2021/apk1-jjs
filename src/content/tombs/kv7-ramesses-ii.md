One of the largest tombs in the valley, KV7 was visible in antiquity but has been repeatedly flooded, leaving its decoration badly damaged. A French mission led by Christian Leblanc worked for decades to clear and study it.

Ramesses II's mummy was later moved to the royal cache at Deir el-Bahari and is now displayed in the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization in Cairo.
